# Story 44: Charging feedback

## Story

44. As a user, I want an exterior battery-coloured glow around the notch while power is connected so that I can see charging and charge level at a glance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given a present battery is connected and charging, when I view the idle notch, then a layered, softly shadowed glow surrounds its exterior edge and pulses continuously without covering its black interior; the colour moves from red through orange to green as charge rises, reaching green at 80% and remaining green through 100%.
- [ ] Given a present battery is connected but not charging, when I view the idle notch, then the exterior glow remains steady at the colour for the current charge, including steady green at the 80% limit or full charge; when disconnected or no battery is present, the charging glow disappears.
- [ ] Given the notch starts while connected or battery state changes, when I use the notch or play music, then the glow reflects the current state without requiring a new connection, the existing media and notch interactions remain usable, and returning from another view shows the current state without a stale connection effect.

## Plan

- Map the present, connected, charging and percentage states to a glow colour and motion state.
- Render a softly graduated exterior glow around the idle notch without covering its black interior or changing its input area.
- Preserve the current media priority and existing notch interactions as the battery state changes.
- Cover charge colours, state transitions and interruption with automated checks and review the visual result in a live session.

## Out of scope

- Low-battery breathing glow while unplugged, workspace fly animation, expanded cross-fade and OLED pixel shift (stories #45–#48).
- The independent decorative edge (story #49), notification glow (story #39), critical notifications and changes to the media priority.
- Charging feedback on the lock screen or greeter.

## Suggestions (your call)

- The existing centre shape clips its children, so an exterior glow needs room outside that shape without expanding the clickable area.
- The battery service distinguishes charging, full and plugged-but-not-charging states; the latter includes charge-limited operation.
